What Would Change Without IT?

Simulating multi-sectorial impacts across Chennai's socio-economic fabric.

Employment Shifts

Engineers would pivot into non-tech manufacturing, civil engineering projects, public administration, and specialized technical trades.

Real Estate Corrections

Rental demand along OMR and Velachery would recalibrate towards affordable housing, healthcare facilities, and research centers.

Traffic Re-Distribution

Peak traffic congestion on the IT corridor would balance evenly across industrial belts like Sriperumbudur, Guindy, and Ennore.

Local Service Ecosystems

Restaurants, cafeterias, cab fleets, and local retail serving tech parks would adapt by shifting service offerings to local residents.

Educational Pivot

Universities would broaden engineering curricula beyond Computer Science, boosting Mechanical, Electronics, Chemical, and Biotech programs.

Startup Diversification

Entrepreneurs would focus on solving physical challenges: deep-tech hardware, clean energy grids, agricultural logistics, and medical tech.
